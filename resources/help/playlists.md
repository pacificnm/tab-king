# Play lists and favorites

## Favorites

Click the heart on any song (in the tree, in a list, or on the song's page). **Favorites** in the menu lists them all.

## Play lists

- **Play Lists** in the menu shows your play lists. Create one with **New play list…**, or right-click **Play Lists** in the menu.
- Add songs from a song's page (**Add to play list…**), from the right-click menu, or from inside the play list (**Add songs…**).
- Reorder by dragging a song, or with the **↑** and **↓** buttons on each row.
- Rename or delete a play list from its right-click menu. Deleting a play list never deletes the songs.

## Playing in a row

Playing an album, an artist, a play list, the favorites or a set of search results lines the songs up. When one ends, the next starts by itself. The footer shows **Previous song**, **Next song** and your place in the queue. A song whose file is missing is skipped, with a message.
