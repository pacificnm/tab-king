# Adding songs

Choose **Add song…** (on the home screen, or by right-clicking **Artists**, an artist or an album in the menu).

## What you need

- A **Guitar Pro file** (.gp, .gp3, .gp4, .gp5 or .gpx). The title, artist, album and track list are read from it.
- Optionally a **master MP3**: a recording of the whole song. Its tags (title, artist, album, year, cover art) are used to fill in the form.
- Optionally a **MIDI file** to use instead of the sound generated from the tab.
- Optionally one **MP3 per track** (a "stem"), to hear a real recording of that instrument.

## Tips

- Check the Artist, Album and Title fields before saving: songs are grouped by them.
- Files are **copied** into the library folder. Deleting a song can remove its copies too; you are asked.
- Use **Edit** on a song's page to replace files later. Right-click an artist or album and choose **Edit artist…** or **Edit album…** to rename it.
- If a file goes missing from the library folder, the song's page shows which one. Edit the song to choose it again.

See [MP3 audio and sync](help:mp3-sync) for lining a recording up with the tab.
