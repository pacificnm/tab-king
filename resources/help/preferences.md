# Preferences

Open **File → Preferences**. Changes take effect straight away.

## Appearance

Choose **System** (follows your operating system), **Light**, **Dark**, or one of the colour themes **Midnight** and **Amber**.

## Locations

- **Library folder**: where Tab King keeps copies of your song files. When you change it you are offered to **copy the files** to the new folder; afterwards you can remove the old copies. The new folder must be empty (or already a Tab King library).
- **Backup folder**: where backups are written. See [Backup and restore](help:backup-restore).

## Audio

- **Output device** for all sound. If the saved device is unplugged, the system default is used.
- **Metronome** and **Count-in** can be switched on whenever a song opens.
- **SoundFont**: choose a .sf2 or .sf3 file to change how the synthesizer sounds, or go back to the built-in bank.
