# Getting started

Tab King plays guitar tablature from Guitar Pro files, with an optional MP3 recording that stays in step with the tab. Everything is stored on your computer and works offline.

## The basics

1. Choose **Add song…** on the home screen to add a Guitar Pro file (see [Adding songs](help:adding-songs)).
2. Open the menu (the **☰** button at the top left) to browse your library by Artist, Album and Song, to search, and to reach Play Lists and Favorites.
3. Double-click a song, or press **Play** on its page, to open it in the player.

## Where things are

- **File** menu: Preferences, and Backup / Restore.
- **Help** menu: this help, and About (version and update check).
- The **footer** is always visible and holds the transport controls: play, stop, previous and next, speed, metronome, count-in and loop.

> Your songs are copied into a library folder that Tab King manages, so you can move or delete the original files afterwards.
