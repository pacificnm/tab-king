# MP3 audio and sync

A recording rarely lines up exactly with a tab. Tab King lets you line them up, and then keeps the cursor and the audio together through seeking, looping and speed changes.

## Open the sync editor

On a song with an MP3, choose **Sync audio…** in the player. You see the waveform with a line for each bar.

## Start offset

The **start offset** says where bar 1 begins in the recording. Use the nudge buttons (±10 ms, ±100 ms), type a value, or play to the right moment and choose **Set to playhead**. A negative offset is for recordings that start after bar 1 of the tab.

## Sync points

If the recording speeds up or slows down compared to the tab, add **sync points**: play to the start of a bar, choose that bar, and choose **Set bar here**. Between points the timing is spread evenly, and tempo changes in the tab are respected. Use **Play from bar** to check your work.

The same sync applies to the master MP3 and every track MP3 of the song.

Edits take effect immediately so you can listen; **Save** keeps them and **Cancel** goes back to what was saved.
