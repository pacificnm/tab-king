# Browsing and searching

## The library tree

Open the menu with **☰**. **Artists** expands to Albums, and Albums to Songs. Use the arrow keys to move, **→** and **←** to expand and collapse, and **Enter** to play the focused song. Right-click (or press **Shift+F10**) for actions such as editing, deleting, or adding to a play list.

Click the heart beside a song to mark it as a favorite.

## Search

Choose **Search** in the menu and start typing. Results appear as you type, in three groups:

- **Songs** matching the title, artist, album or genre;
- **Albums** and **Artists** whose names match.

Search ignores capital letters and accents, and matches the beginning of words, so `mov pic` finds _Moving Pictures_. Open an album or artist from the results to see its songs.

Playing from a list of results queues the whole list.
