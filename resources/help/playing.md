# Playing a tab

The player shows the notation and tablature with a cursor that follows the music and scrolls with it.

- **Click** a bar or beat to move there.
- **Play/Pause** (or **Space**) starts and stops; **Stop** returns to the start.
- The **seek bar** in the footer moves through the whole song; the readout shows time and bar number.
- **Previous measure** and **Next measure** step by a bar.
- **Zoom in/out** and the **View** switch (page or horizontal scroll) change how the tab is laid out.

## What you hear

By default the tab is played by the built-in synthesizer. If the song has a master MP3 or track MP3s, you can choose per track which one to hear: see [Tracks and mixing](help:tracks).

You can change the synthesizer's SoundFont and the output device in [Preferences](help:preferences).
