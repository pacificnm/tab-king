# Practice tools

## Speed

The **−** and **+** buttons change the tempo in steps of 5% from 25% to 200%; **Reset** returns to 100%. MP3 audio is stretched without changing its pitch.

## Metronome and count-in

- **Metronome** adds a click on every beat; it has its own volume slider.
- **Count-in** plays three clicks at the song's tempo before playback starts.

Both can start switched on every time you open a song: see [Preferences](help:preferences).

## Loop a passage

1. Drag across the bars you want, type the first and last bar in **Bars**, or pick a **Section** from the list.
2. Choose **Loop** (or press **L**).

The loop stays active when you seek, until you clear the selection.

Try looping a hard passage at 60% speed with the count-in on, then raise the speed a step at a time.
